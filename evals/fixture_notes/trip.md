## Meeting point

Meet at the clock outside Willow Station. The shuttle leaves at 07:40. Everyone needs a paper ticket to board.

## Overnight stop

The cabin booking is under Rowan Vale. Sleeping bags are provided, so bring a pillowcase. Hang damp coats on the rail in the porch.

## Return journey

On Sunday the return coach departs from the old mill. The last ferry crossing leaves at 18:15. Keep the blue voucher for the ferry crew.
