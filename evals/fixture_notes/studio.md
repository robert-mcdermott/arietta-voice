## Keys

The spare studio key stays in the black envelope behind reception. The building entrance locks at eight in the evening.

## Lending desk

Aprons can be borrowed until closing time. Sign the apron register at the counter and return each apron to the striped basket.

## Supplies

Printing ink lives in the grey cabinet. Put used rollers in the shallow tray. Blank postcards come in packs of twenty.

## Deliveries

Paper deliveries go to the side entrance on Birch Lane. The supplier needs four working days to deliver a fresh box.
