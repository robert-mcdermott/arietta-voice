# House manual

Made-up notes for benchmarks. Each section is longer than one note, and its
last facts sit past the first 420 characters.

## Heating and hot water

The house is heated by the gas boiler in the garage, which also heats the hot
water. The thermostat in the hall controls every room except the attic, which
has its own electric heater on a timer. In winter the schedule keeps the house
at 19 degrees from 6 am to 10 pm and at 16 degrees overnight, and the holiday
setting holds it at 12 degrees while everyone is away. The radiators were bled
last autumn and do not need attention until next year. If the hot water runs
cold, press the reset button on the left side of the boiler and hold it for
five seconds. The emergency gas shutoff valve is behind the yellow panel by the
back door.

## Alarm system

The alarm panel is in the hall closet, to the right of the coats. The system
covers the front and back doors, the garage door and the ground floor windows,
and the motion sensor in the living room is switched off at night so the cat
does not set it off. When the alarm is armed, the panel shows a red light and
beeps twice when a door opens, and there are thirty seconds to switch it off.
The batteries in the window sensors were changed in March. To silence a false
alarm, press Off twice and then the green button. The monitoring company is
Brightline Security.

## Garden

The front garden is mostly lavender and rosemary, which need almost no water
once they are established. The vegetable beds behind the house have tomatoes,
beans and courgettes in summer and garlic over the winter, and the greenhouse
holds the chilli plants and seedlings. Slug pellets are kept on the top shelf
of the shed, out of reach of the cat. The irrigation for the vegetable beds
runs at 5 am on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays. The garden waste bin is
collected on the first Friday of each month. The compost heap behind the shed
should be turned every two weeks.
