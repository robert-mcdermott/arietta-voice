## Keys

The spare workshop key is in the red tin under the sink. Only the evening steward signs it out.

## Lending desk

To borrow a drill, show your membership card. Return borrowed equipment by noon on Saturday. Overdue loans cost two tokens per day.

## Repairs

Leave broken tools on the orange cart. The repair log is in the purple binder by the vise. Mara checks the cart each Wednesday.

## Supplies

Safety goggles hang beside the blue lockers. Replacement sanding discs go in drawer C. Rechargeable batteries belong in the green charging cradle.
